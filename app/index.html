About this ledger

This is a fan-made guide and tracker for the 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list. The guides are written in my own words from those descriptions and from the cited guide sites — PowerPyx, TrueTrophies, Exophase and PSNProfiles — with a link to the source on every trophy’s guide panel.

How to use it

  • Tick a trophy’s checkbox when you earn it. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and survives a reload.
  • Use Export progress to save your ticks to a file, and Import progress to bring them back on another device.
  • Click Guide on any row to open its full guide: steps, missable note, and the cited source.
  • Spoiler-safe hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
  • There are no online trophies in this list — the online filter shows nothing, which is correct.

Roadmap facts (PowerPyx)

  • Difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours to platinum; the hardest trophy is A Long Road (100% completion).
  • 55 offline trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), 0 online, 1 playthrough minimum.
  • Story is 5 chapters, about 60–80 hours. Free-roam continues after the story, but there is no mission replay.
  • Cheats disable trophies — do not use them on a trophy run.
  • The 100% Completion Checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) is about 60% of true game completion: 70 of 80 Challenges, 15 of 18 card sets, and the full robberies, shooting ranges, drone races, underwater discoveries and Bret’s quests.

Honesty note

A few triggers were not yet confirmed by the guide sites when this ledger was written — Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck I Guess and The Truth Is Out There. Their guides say so plainly and follow the official description rather than guessing. The missable list is honest about what is confirmed and what is flagged as a one-time moment.
