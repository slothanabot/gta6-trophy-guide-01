Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). There is no mission replay and no New Game+, but free-roam continues after the story — so play the story first, then clean up. Keep cheats off the whole run: they disable trophies and saving.

    The 100% checklist, in numbers

    It appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story, but progress made during the story already counts. It is roughly 60% true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 50 of 66 weapons, 70 of 80 challenges. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need everything. (PowerPyx)

    Missable trophies — 14 to watch

    GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game+, so a one-time story beat or choice you pass without doing cannot be redone on this save. PowerPyx (November 2026) confirms only one trophy as potentially missable — the Chapter 1 meditation. The other thirteen below are flagged because they hang on a single story moment, a choice, or a side strand that can lock out. Do them when they appear.

      About this ledger

      A free, open-source, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files — no network, no account, no ads — and your progress never leaves your browser unless you export it.

      How to use it

      • Tick trophies off as you earn them; progress saves in localStorage automatically.
      • Export downloads your progress as JSON; Import restores it on another machine.
      • Filters narrow the ledger by story, missable, collectibles, activities or online. Search matches names, descriptions and guide text.
      • Spoiler-safe hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
      • Click any trophy row to open its full guide — steps, missable note and source.
      • Deep links work: #trophy-17 opens a guide, ?tab=roadmap opens the roadmap, ?demo=1 previews the ledger with sample progress.
