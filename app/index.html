      How to Use This Ledger

      • Tick trophies off as you earn them. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and survives closing the page.
      • Export your progress to a file and import it back on another device or after clearing browser data.
      • Hide story spoilers to blank out the names and descriptions of story trophies until you have earned them.
      • Filter by kind — story, missable, collectibles, activities — or search by name or mission.
      • Sources: every guide names its source (Exophase, PSNProfiles, PowerPyx or TrueTrophies) beside the steps it supports. Guides are written in this ledger's own words; official trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them.
      • Fully offline: this page loads no network resources. It works with the network off.

      Sources consulted: Exophase trophy list, PSNProfiles trophy list, PowerPyx GTA 6 trophy guide, TrueTrophies trophy list, and Rockstar Newswire / official GTA VI pages. Each trophy's guide cites the source beside it.
